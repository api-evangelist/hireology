# Hireology

> Hireology is a purpose-built applicant tracking system (ATS) and hiring platform for multi-location, high-volume operators. It covers candidate sourcing, engagement, screening, evaluation, hiring decisions, onboarding handoff, and hiring analytics, with an integration ecosystem spanning payroll, HRIS, background checks, and job boards. Hireology does not publish a public developer API portal or OpenAPI specification; integrations are delivered through named partner connectors.
